Pricing Guide

How Modular Interior Cost is Calculated

Key factors that determine the pricing of your Ahmedabad home interior.

Square Footage (Carpet Area)

Wardrobes and TV units are calculated per square foot of frontage, while kitchens are calculated per running/linear foot.

Core Substrate Selection

BWP Marine Plywood (IS:710) is recommended for wet areas, while HDHMR and BWR ply are used for dry wardrobes and bedroom units.

Hardware & Accessories

Tandem soft-close drawers, lift-up systems, pantry pull-outs, and corner carousels determine functional convenience and cost.
