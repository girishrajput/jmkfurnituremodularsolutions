What makes factory-made modular furniture better than carpenter-made furniture?
Factory-made modular furniture is cut using computerized CNC panel saws with 0.1mm precision and edge-banded with 200°C PUR hot-melt glue that makes all board edges 100% waterproof and moisture sealed. Carpenters cut manually with hand tools, leaving rough edges where water can enter, causing peeling and termite infestations. Modular units are also flat-packed and assembled in days without months of dust in your home.
What is the typical cost for 2 BHK and 3 BHK full home interior in Ahmedabad?
In Ahmedabad, a standard 2 BHK turnkey interior package (modular kitchen, 2 wardrobes, TV unit, foyer unit, false ceiling & paint) starts around ₹3.85 Lakhs to ₹5.5 Lakhs. A 3 BHK luxury turnkey package ranges between ₹5.85 Lakhs to ₹9 Lakhs depending on choice of finishes (Laminate vs Acrylic vs PU) and hardware brands.
What is the difference between BWP Plywood and HDHMR?
BWP (Boiling Water Proof IS:710) Marine Plywood is made of cross-bonded timber veneers bonded with synthetic phenol formaldehyde resin, offering the highest strength and moisture resistance for wet zones (kitchen sink areas and vanity units). HDHMR (High Density High Moisture Resistance) is an engineered wood board with high density (>850 kg/m³), perfect for precision CNC routing, smooth acrylic finishes, and wardrobe shutters.
How does the 10-Year Warranty work?
JMK provides a written 10-Year Warranty against borer, termite infestation, and core delamination on all our factory-grade plywood and HDHMR woodwork. Hardware fittings (Blum, Hettich, Hafele) carry their respective lifetime manufacturer warranties for smooth operation.
Can I customize the dimensions and internal wardrobe configurations?
Yes, 100%! Every single modular cabinet, wardrobe drawer, shelf height, and pantry unit is tailor-made to your exact wall dimensions and personal storage requirements.
