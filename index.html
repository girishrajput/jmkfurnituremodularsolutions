Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our modular furniture manufacturing, materials, and pricing in Ahmedabad.

What is the starting price for a modular kitchen in Ahmedabad?
A standard L-shaped or straight modular kitchen by JMK starts at approximately ₹1,45,000 for high-grade HDHMR/Marine Ply carcass with 1mm anti-scratch laminate and soft-close wire basket accessories. Acrylic and PU finish kitchens range from ₹2.2 Lakh to ₹4.5 Lakh depending on size and accessories.
What materials do you use for moisture and termite protection?
We strictly use 100% boiling waterproof (BWP IS:710) Marine Plywood and Greenpanel/Action TESA HDHMR boards for wet areas (kitchens and bathroom vanities). All materials are 100% borer and termite resistant with manufacturer certification.
How much time does turnkey home interior execution take?
Because our modular components are manufactured in-house at our Ahmedabad workshop using CNC machinery, we deliver full 2 BHK and 3 BHK turnkey projects within 35 to 45 business days from 3D design finalization.
Do you charge for site visits and 3D design consultation in Ahmedabad?
No! Initial site laser measurement, consultation, and preliminary 3D floor plan layout are completely FREE for homeowners and business owners across Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar.
What kind of warranty does JMK Furniture Modular Solutions offer?
We provide an official 10-Year Warranty against termite, borer, and delamination on all core ply/HDHMR woodwork, alongside manufacturer warranty on Blum/Hettich hardware fittings.
