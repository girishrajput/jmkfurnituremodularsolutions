Core Substrates

Plywood & Board Grades We Use

We never use cheap particle board or low-grade MDF. Every board is selected for durability and moisture resistance.

BWP IS:710 Marine Plywood

Boiling Water Proof: Uses un-extended phenol formaldehyde resin. Withstands 72 hours of boiling water testing. Essential for kitchen sink cabinets and wet bathroom vanities.

Action TESA / Greenpanel HDHMR

High Density High Moisture Resistance: Density >850 kg/m³. Perfectly flat surface ideal for CNC routing, acrylic pressing, and smooth zero-chipping modular shutters.

BWR IS:303 Commercial Ply

Boiling Water Resistant: Calibrated plywood used for dry room wardrobes, TV units, beds, and study desks. 100% borer and termite treated with vacuum pressure impregnation.
