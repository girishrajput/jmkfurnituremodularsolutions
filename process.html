Transparent Workflow

Our Hassle-Free 5-Step Execution Process

A systematic, stress-free interior journey from initial sketch to move-in celebration.

Home / Our Process
1

Site Measurement & Lifestyle Discovery

Our senior interior designer visits your flat, bungalow, or commercial office anywhere in Ahmedabad. We take laser-precision digital measurements of every wall, beam, electrical outlet, and plumbing line while understanding your storage preferences, color palettes, and budget.

Duration: Day 1 - 2
2

3D Photorealistic Design & Material Selection

We translate your requirements into realistic 3D computer visualizations. You get to see exact lighting, colors, finishes, and spatial ergonomics before spending a single rupee. We provide an itemized quote with zero hidden charges.

Duration: Day 3 - 7
3

Precision Factory CNC Manufacturing

Once the design is locked, modular cabinet codes are fed into our automated German beam saws and edge-banders in Ahmedabad. Every panel is edge-sealed with moisture-proof PUR glue at 200°C to guarantee extreme longevity.

Duration: Day 8 - 30
4

Clean, Dust-Free On-Site Installation

Pre-finished modular units are securely delivered to your site. Our trained technicians assemble cabinets using Minifix connectors and soft-close hinges with minimal noise and virtually zero on-site sawdust or mess.

Duration: Day 31 - 42
5

Quality Audit, Deep Cleaning & 10-Yr Warranty Handover

A 42-point quality check is conducted by our quality engineer. We conduct a thorough deep cleaning and hand over the keys along with official 10-Year Warranty documentation and post-installation service guarantees.

Duration: Day 43 - 45
